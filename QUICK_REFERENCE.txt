🔢 SCORING
──────────

Compliance:        Score = (PKT×0.4) + (Att×0.2) + (Prod×0.2) + (Audit×0.2)
Final Clearance:   Score = (PKT×0.3) + (SLA×0.3) + (Count×0.2) + (Audit×0.2)
Internal Audit:    Score = (QMG×0.4) + (Time×0.2) + (Complete×0.2) + (Att×0.2)
Paperwork:         Score = (SLA×0.3) + (Alloc×0.2) + (Audit×0.2) + (PKT×0.15) + (Att×0.15)
HR Operations:     Score = Average of all team scores

Color:             🟢≥90 (Green) | 🟡80-89 (Amber) | 🔴<80 (Red)

Eligibility:       ✅ Eligible IF NH Pending=0 AND Client Pending=0
                   ❌ Not Eligible IF NH Pending>0 OR Client Pending>0
